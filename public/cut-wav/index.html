Cut WAV files online (browser only)

Trim WAV audio locally in the browser and export MP3 or WAV with Free MP3 Cutter.

Steps

  1. Load a WAV file. Use drag-and-drop on the Free MP3 Cutter home page.
  2. Trim on the waveform. Set start and end points in the manual editor. WAV is large; allow extra time for processing.
  3. Download. Export defaults to MP3 for compatibility. Re-encode may take longer than stream-copy cuts.

Large files

WAV files can be hundreds of megabytes. The tool warns above 1 GB and blocks above 2 GB to protect your browser.
